First impressions of Meridian 2 after a week of long-context testing

Notes

I spent a week putting entire codebases into the new model. It handled a 600,000 token prompt without losing track of early files, but it was slow to start answering.

For short tasks it was no better than the previous model, and it cost more per request because of the longer prompts I was tempted to send.

Written for the test suite; not a real article.

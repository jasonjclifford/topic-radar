Claude Code Max plans move to weekly usage limits with new per-model caps

The change

Starting December 1, the five-hour usage window on the Max plans is replaced by a weekly cap. The 100 dollar plan gets 240 hours of the mid-size model and 24 hours of the large model each week, and the 200 dollar plan gets four times that.

Monthly prices do not change. The vendor says about one subscriber in twenty will reach a cap in a normal week, mostly people who run agents in the background around the clock.

What counts toward the cap

Cached input tokens now count at one tenth of their normal weight toward the weekly cap, so sessions that reuse a long instruction file stretch much further than before.

Extra usage can be bought once a cap is reached, at 3 dollars per additional hour of the mid-size model.

What to do

Keep an eye on the new usage page, which shows hours used per model and resets every Monday at 09:00 UTC. Moving routine edits to the mid-size model is the simplest way to stay under the large-model cap.
